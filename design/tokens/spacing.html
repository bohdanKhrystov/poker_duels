Spacing

One ladder from 2 to 64. Related things sit at 8–12, sections at 24–32, screens breathe at 48–64. Depth is drawn with hairlines, not shadows — one shadow exists, for the things that float.

Only menus and dialogs float.

Flat surfaces never carry a shadow: a seat plate, a list row and the table all sit on their hairline. The one shadow marks the one thing temporarily above the game.

Focus

--pd-focus — a 2px gap, then 2px of accent — on every focusable thing, keyboard first. The ring is the accent's job; it is never decoration.
