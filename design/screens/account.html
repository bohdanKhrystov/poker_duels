What a browser sees once its own profile can speak for itself. The screen states, in words, which routes currently sign in — the device, and the password once one exists — never inferring either past a read the client actually got back (ADR-0037). The same panel carries the form that gives a profile its first password, the door to an account already made, and the address a forgotten password could still recover through.

recovery-text.ts's own words — the why-line, the two field labels and the button — offered whether recovery is already on or off, since hiding it once an address is attached would strand a player whose address stopped working (AccountScreen.tsx). The condition that composes it onto a page does not check whether that device has a password yet, so it renders on both frames above exactly as it does here; whether it belongs on the no-password one is DEC-090, open — this card draws what ships (ADR-0091 §3) and settles nothing. A settled attempt reads one of four sentences from the same module — acknowledged, address refused, wrong password, or a generic failure — not drawn as their own frames; the three states above are the ones this ticket names.
