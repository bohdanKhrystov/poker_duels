Seat & pot

A seat is a quiet plate: name, stack, and — when it matters — whose move it is. The turn is marked by an accent edge that is always reserved, so nothing on the table ever jumps. A second mark shows the most recent act of the hand, at the seat that made it, worded exactly as that seat's own button would say it — one mark, never one per seat.
